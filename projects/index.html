  • WA

    NWIS essential system services headroom study

    Pilbara ISOCo · responsible for technical execution

    • Built the RMS dynamic model in PowerFactory from Pilbara ISOCo's full model and the facility operators' machine models, validated it against a recorded contingency, then reduced it to a single-bus model validated against the full network.
    • Defined the credible scenarios (loss of the largest unit, a line or a network area) with merit-order dispatch inside each network service provider's portfolio.
    • Designed and scripted a bisection search on dispatch to find the least headroom that keeps the frequency nadir above 49 Hz, as the Harmonised Technical Rules ask.
    • Checked the results with sense checks, hand calculations of RoCoF, a brute-force comparison and manual reruns.

    result Showed Pilbara ISOCo could cut its headroom procurement from 200 MW to 60 MW. The report became the basis of its procurement.

  • NT

    Darwin-Katherine Electricity System generation portfolio optimisation

    NT Department of Mining and Energy · responsible for the optimisation modelling

    • Sized solar (MW) and batteries (MWh) for the lowest LCOE over the long run, with half-hourly power balance, thermal unit and battery constraints, and LGC revenue.
    • Built EGPO in Python for it: a Bayesian optimiser, a half-hourly dispatch calculator and an LCOE calculator. Benchmarked it against HOMER and confirmed the optimum with a brute-force sweep.
    • Sensitivities on fuel price, IRR and LGC price, with the break-even fuel price.
    • Recommended a synchronous condenser to replace the system strength and inertia of the retiring units.

    result An optimal portfolio of 242 MW of solar (committed solar included) and at least 50 MWh of battery as spinning reserve, cheaper than replenishing the thermal fleet. It became the economic basis of the Detailed Business Case for the Darwin Renewable Energy Hub.

  • TAS

    Power factor correction at Renison Bell

    Bluestone Mines Tasmania JV, an underground tin mine near Zeehan

    • Compared a capacitor bank, an SVC, a STATCOM and a synchronous condenser, and chose a stepped capacitor bank.
    • Placed it near the remote end, where Newton-Raphson load flows showed the lowest voltage.
    • Sized it with a sweep through the PowerFactory Python API for near-unity power factor at the 44 kV connection point, with every bus within 0.95 to 1.05 p.u.
    • Went underground to check it could be built, then wrote the feasibility report and preliminary design.

    result The worst bus rose from 0.8 p.u. into the band. The capacitor bank was installed and is in operation.
